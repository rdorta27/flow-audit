Orders cost about 299.2 h of manual rework a month

Flow Audit read 5,000 orders from Order manager, Activation system (3 sources) and looked for 3 patterns that make people redo work by hand. Measured: 5,000 orders, 720 with a finding. Projected: 299.2 h a month, from minutes you can change; not money, not a booked loss.

Where the hours go

  1. Activation fails and nobody retries (R01) 283.3 h in 400 orders
  2. No end-to-end correlation id (R05) 225 h in 300 orders
  3. Duplicate service order (R04) 30 h in 20 orders

What it could not see. Product catalog and Process orchestrator were not read. Order manager: when each state change happened: the source is a snapshot, not a history. Activation system: when each attempt changed state: the source is a snapshot, not a history.
